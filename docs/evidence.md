# Evidence and assumption provenance (24 Sep 2026)

- AMD MI355X primary product page: 288 GB HBM3E, 8 TB/s, 1400 W board, and different precision peaks. https://www.amd.com/en/products/accelerators/instinct/mi350/mi355x.html
- Micron HBM4 primary product page: 36 GB 12-high production class; 48 GB 16-high samples; >2.8 TB/s 36 GB stack claim. https://www.micron.com/products/memory/hbm/hbm4
- TSMC advanced packaging primary page: CoWoS-S 3.3 reticle/2700 mm² class; CoWoS-L/R for larger interposers. https://3dfabric.tsmc.com/english/dedicatedFoundry/technology/cowos.htm

These references motivate feasibility envelopes. AIM die area, clock, power, coherent four-module fabric, per-link bandwidth, utilization, tile dataflow and deliverable timing are original design assumptions. Supplier proof for a four-module coherent 512 GB device is not implied by these references.
